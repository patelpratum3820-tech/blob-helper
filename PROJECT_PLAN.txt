1. VISION OF THIS PROJECT:
   Blob AI is your living, floating 3D desktop companion ("Jarvis"). It stays 
   on top of your Windows screen without frames or borders, talks with personality,
   moves and expresses emotions in real-time 3D, and listens directly through an
   in-built microphone to execute actions or chat via local Ollama AI.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ANSWERING YOUR QUESTIONS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[QUESTION 1]: Is it possible to change Blob from 2D to real 3D that moves around 
              and has emotions (not frame-by-frame)?
[ANSWER]: YES! 100% POSSIBAL!
   - Why 2D is limited: Right now, blob.py uses 2D pixel circle drawing (QPainter).
   - How 3D works:
     * We use a real 3D mesh (Three.js/WebGL or ModernGL/OpenGL) inside a transparent
       floating PyQt window.
     * Dynamic squish & jelly physics: Vertex displacement shader gives Blob organic,
       jiggly, bouncy fluid motion at 60 FPS.
     * 3D Emotions & Morphs:
       - Idle: Floating gently, subtle breathing, natural 3D blinks.
       - Thinking: Tilts up looking at the sky, golden pulsing wave.
       - Happy: Bouncy squash-and-stretch, smiling 3D eye morphs.
       - Surprised: Elastic stretch, eyes widen, mouth opens in 3D.
       - Typing: Cyan glow, rhythmic forward lean.
     * Real-time 3D Eye Tracking: The 3D eyes can physically follow your mouse cursor
       across your monitor!
     * You can also load any custom 3D character (.glb / .gltf model) from Blender!

[QUESTION 2]: Is it possible to build an in-built mic for voice-to-chat instead of 
              pressing Windows + H?
[ANSWER]: YES! 100% POSSIBAL!
   - Currently you have to press Win + H (Windows speech dictation).
   - With in-built microphone integration:
     * A cute mic button on Blob or push-to-talk hotkey (e.g. hold Space).
     * Python audio listener records speech directly from your microphone.
     * Speech-to-Text converts your voice into text automatically.
     * The prompt is sent straight to Ollama (qwen2.5:3b) without typing anything!
     * Bonus: We can add Text-to-Speech (TTS) so Jarvis can speak back with a cute voice!

[QUESTION 3]: How do I upload this project to GitHub with Ollama setup and model download?
[ANSWER]: CRITICAL WARNING & EASY SOLUTION:
